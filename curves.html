How to read the path

The path follows transition-profile similarity. Zero begins at the group 1 end and one reaches the Bio side. These are relative positions, not dates or elapsed time.

The proposed curve was initialized to allow the Early A return and then fitted freely in all 254 positive MDS dimensions. Other routes fit almost as well. The sequence is evidence for a proposed chronology, not an independently established historical order.

Only eight axes are displayed. Positions and distances from the path retain the full-space calculation. A page can project onto the middle of the path while remaining far from it.
