# Concise forum methodology

I tested the stability of the proposed ordering using 207 paragraph profiles and 50 pooled bifolia, with the transition-distance/MDS representation held fixed. Following ElPiGraph's ensemble procedure [1], I fitted curves to 100 random approximately 90% subsets, using eight starting routes and two regularization settings; a whole-bifolium sampling control brought the total to 4,800 fits. I recorded recurring positions and order reversals, and compared competing routes' scores within the same sample. For the H map, I applied Cassidy's implicit-derivative continuation [2] to move observed H:58–65 through MDS1/2 and MDS1/3 at lambda = 0.01, correcting at assignment changes and checking different movement paths. Finally, I ran the original scEGRET R functions [3] on 27 candidate orders per cohort, varying bin resolution, word/line features and count scaling (648 evaluations), to identify abrupt feature changes. These measure sensitivity and continuity conditional on the model—not historical probabilities, dates, or the locations of missing bifolia. Mixed or excluded scEGRET bins can leave an ordering unresolved.

Following scEGRET's validation logic [3], I then used continuous trajectories and deliberate missing-state controls to calibrate the feature-change scores: 147,888 production simulations, with held-out false-alarm and power checks. The manuscript-specific sheet-resampling and Dirichlet–multinomial noise models, scan adjustment and supplementary two-sided diagnostic are adaptations developed for this analysis. A follow-up applied seven actual-page removal masks to the observed data and 7,596 regenerated controls, recomputing feature eligibility and native bins while keeping the original Golf ordering fixed.

The calibration does not robustly detect a discontinuity at f58, but its limited power cannot establish continuity. Removing f58 produces flags in some raw-count or alternative-model comparisons, not in normalized comparisons under sheet resampling. Because removing either f58 or its bin companions excludes the sparse focal bin, the test cannot isolate f58 as the surviving bridge. I repeated the ElPiGraph ensemble with RF1b-er using individual basic EVA characters: another 4,800 fits with matched pages, bifolia, masks, starts and penalties. The original ZL representation uses Zattera glyph groups, so this tests the combined effect of transcription and unitization. Separate charts and a 50:50 average of the retained-fit position histograms preserve alternative placements. The 3a/3b ambiguity persists; among best candidates with 1a before 1b, the pooled λ=0.01 preference changes from 58/100 samples placing 3a first in ZL to 43/100 in RF. These frequencies describe sensitivity under the tested settings, not historical probabilities. [Full comparison](rf1b.html).
